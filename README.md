Cleave: Smart Expense Tracker

Cleave is a modern, privacy-focused fintech application designed to help users take full control of their financial life. It combines personal budget planning with seamless group expense sharing, all while ensuring that sensitive financial data never leaves the device.

🚀 The Most Awaited Feature is Here!
With the latest v1.5.0 update, Cleave now supports Custom Categories. You can finally create your own spending categories with personalized icons and colors to match your unique lifestyle.

✨ Key Features
📊 Personal Budgeting
• Monthly Spend Goals: Set an overall target for your monthly expenses.
• Category-wise Limits: Allocate specific budgets for Food, Transport, Shopping, and your new Custom Categories.
• Budget Health Pulse: Real-time indicators that alert you if your spending pace is exceeding your plan.
• Investment Tracking: A dedicated category to monitor your wealth-building activities like stocks, SIPs, and mutual funds.

🤖 Automation & Intelligence
• Auto-Tracking: Cleave reads bank SMS/Notification alerts to log expenses automatically as they happen.
• Receipt Scanning: Powered by Google ML Kit, instantly convert physical receipts into digital entries using high-accuracy OCR.
• Smart Categorization: An intelligent engine that predicts categories based on merchant names and descriptions.

👥 Group Settlements
• Shared Groups: Create groups for roommates, trips, or events.
• Expense Splitting: Log group spends and see exactly who owes what.
• One-Tap Settle Up: Simplify complex debts into clear, easy-to-pay instructions.

🏠 Seamless Integration
• Home Screen Widgets: Track your remaining budget and log quick expenses without opening the app.
• Privacy Mode: Mask your sensitive balances with a single tap.
• Dynamic Greetings: A personalized home screen that updates based on the time of day.

🛡️ Privacy & Security
• Privacy First: No cloud, no servers. All data processing (including notification reading and OCR) happens strictly locally on your device.
• Biometric Lock: Secure your financial data with fingerprint or face unlock.
• Local Backups: Export and import your data via CSV at any time.

🛠️ Tech Stack
• UI: 100% Jetpack Compose with Material 3.
• Language: Kotlin.
• Architecture: MVVM with Clean Architecture principles.
• DI: Hilt (Dependency Injection).
• Database: Room (v12 Migration).
• Widgets: Jetpack Glance.
• Background Tasks: WorkManager for periodic spending summaries.
• OCR: Google ML Kit (Text Recognition).

📦 Getting Started
1. Clone the repository.
2. Open in Android Studio Ladybug or newer.
3. Sync Gradle and run on an emulator or physical device (API 26+).
